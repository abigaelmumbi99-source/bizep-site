Bizep Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-04

What this app does

Bizep Social is an internal tool used only by Bizep to create and publish its own content to Bizep's own YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and X accounts.

Information we access

When Bizep's authorised administrator signs in, the app receives permission to upload and publish content to the signed-in accounts and to read basic channel or Page details such as the name. The app does not access the data of any other users.

How information is stored

Sign-in tokens are stored in encrypted form on Bizep's own computer. They are not sent to any third party and are not sold or shared.

Third-party services

Content is published through the official APIs of YouTube (Google), Meta and X, and is subject to those platforms' own terms and privacy policies. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google, Facebook or X account security settings.
